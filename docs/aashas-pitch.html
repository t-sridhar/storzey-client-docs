“Bring us your inspiration, leave with something made around you.”

The one idea we will build the brand on, for six months, across every platform.

What we see

You already have the advantage that is hardest to manufacture.

Customers can bring an idea, sit with a designer, and have an outfit made around them. That is a real advantage over both the large ethnic chains and the Instagram-only labels.

It is currently invisible to anyone who has not already walked in. Our job over six months is to make it visible to the right fifteen kilometres of Hyderabad, consistently, and to turn that attention into booked store appointments.

The measure

We ask to be judged on qualified store appointments, not followers.

Revenue walks through your door. Follower count and view count can both rise while footfall stays flat, so appointments are the number we report against first. We will also report the Hyderabad share of your reach — three thousand views inside the city are worth more to you than a hundred thousand spread across the country.

How it is made

One half-day a month produces most of your content.

We build a fixed filming setup in a corner of the boutique — your collection visible behind — and run one capture session a month with you on camera. Twenty to thirty finished pieces come out of a single sitting. It runs as an interview, not a script: we ask, you answer, and you prepare nothing.

70%Capture session, in-store proof, motion from stills. Low cost, repeatable, and the reason a weekly cadence survives six months rather than three.
30%Model and location shoots. Sets the quality ceiling — and supplies the footage every other piece is cut against.

The thirty per cent is not decoration. A talking head cut against beautiful garment footage looks produced; the same talking head alone looks like a webcam.

Weeks one & two

Foundations, before a single camera comes out.

  • Positioning session; agree the promise and the proof points
  • Build the brand kit: logo rules for video, palette, typefaces, reel intro and outro, caption style
  • Rebuild the Instagram profile: bio, highlights, grid logic, pinned posts
  • Claim and populate the Google Business Profile with photos and correct details
  • Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue and saved replies for price, appointment and shipping
  • Agree who at Aashas answers messages and books appointments, and how fast
  • Write roughly 60 Aashas-specific interview questions from our question bank
  • Build the fixed capture corner in the boutique and photograph the setup card
  • Record the baseline: followers, reach, profile visits, weekly walk-ins, average order value

From week three

The engine runs.

  • One capture session a month: a half-day with the owner producing 20 to 30 short pieces
  • One produced shoot a month: craft, karigars, customisation, real customers
  • A model shoot every second month, batching all model-led content into one day
  • A narrative set piece each quarter, timed to the season
  • Four reels, two carousels and daily stories every week
  • Two collaborator posts a month with bridal makeup artists and wedding photographers
  • Two blog articles a month from month 3, written from the capture session transcripts
  • YouTube long-form from month 3; owner vlogs from month 5
  • Monthly report and planning meeting in the first week of every month
